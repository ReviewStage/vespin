---
title: "How the Inbox works"
description: "Answer your agents' decisions, to-dos, and questions"
---

The Inbox lists the threads agents post when they need something from you. A thread has a title, messages, and usually one ask. Open the Inbox from the sidebar or with <kbd>⇧⌘I</kbd>.

## Asks

### Decide

Pick an option, or press its number, then click **Send**. Some decisions take more than one option.

### To-do

Do the steps, then click **Mark done**. If you can't, click **Couldn't do it** and add a note saying why.

  <img src="/images/inbox-todo.png" alt="A to-do with three steps to verify a mail domain, and Mark done and Couldn't do it buttons" />
</Frame>

### Answer

Write your answer, or fill in the fields, then click **Send**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/inbox-fill-in.png" alt="A form in the Inbox asking for a daily invite limit, who sees pending invites, whether to email the sender, and a reply-to address" />
</Frame>

A thread with no ask is a notice. Click **Got it** to settle it.

## Reply

Write in the box and click **Send** without picking anything. The ask stays open and the agent replies.

## Who a thread waits on

The member the agent addressed it to, else the task's assignee, else whoever started the agent. Anyone in the workspace can answer.

## Filter

**View options** above the list:

- **Questions**: open asks waiting on you.
- **Open**: everything not settled. The default.
- **All**: everything.
- **Reminders**: threads you set a reminder on.
- **Addressed to**: you, anyone, or one teammate.
- **Pinned tasks first**

## Resolve and remind

**Resolve** (<kbd>E</kbd>) settles a thread without answering it. You can undo it until the agent is told.

**Remind me** (<kbd>H</kbd>) hides a thread until the time you pick.

A new post from the agent reopens a settled thread.

## Keyboard

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Inbox | <kbd>⇧⌘I</kbd> |
| Next or previous thread | <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd> |
| Choose an option | <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> |
| Reply to selection | <kbd>R</kbd> |
| Mark as read or unread | <kbd>U</kbd> |
| Remind me | <kbd>H</kbd> |
| Resolve | <kbd>E</kbd> |
| Open in task | <kbd>⌘O</kbd> |

## Notifications

- **Mac and web**: turn on **Show a notification** or **Play a sound** in **Settings › Preferences › Notifications**.
- **iPhone**: threads waiting on you arrive as notifications you can answer.
- **Slack**: see [Slack](/slack).
