The Inbox lists the threads your agents post when they need something from you. Press <kbd>⇧⌘I</kbd> to open it.

## Asks

An agent's message can end in one ask:

- **A decision:** pick one option, or several where it says **Pick any**. Options with pictures add **None of these**.
- **A to-do:** steps you do by hand. **Mark done** when they're done, or **Couldn't do it**.
- **A form:** fields to fill in.
- **An answer:** a reply in your own words.

A message with no ask is a notice. **Got it** acknowledges it.

An ask waits on the member it's addressed to, then the task's assignee, then whoever started the agent. Anyone in the workspace can answer it.

## Answer

Pick, tick, or fill in, add a note if the agent needs one, and **Send**. Press <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> to pick an option and <kbd>⏎</kbd> to send. Send with nothing picked to reply without answering.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/inbox-decide.png" alt="A decision in the Inbox: Who can withdraw an invite?, three options numbered 1 to 3, the third marked Recommended, and Add a note under them" />
</Frame>

Select words in a message and press <kbd>R</kbd> to quote them in your reply.

## Resolve and remind

**Resolve** (<kbd>E</kbd>) closes an item without answering it, and the agent is told. **Remind me** (<kbd>H</kbd>) hides an item until the time you pick, then puts it back at the top. <kbd>⌘Z</kbd> undoes either, though not a Resolve the agent has already been told of.

## What the list shows

**View options** chooses **Questions**, **Open** (the default), **All**, or **Reminders**, and **Addressed to** chooses **Me** (the default), **Anyone**, or one teammate. For every key, see [Keyboard shortcuts](/shortcuts).

## Away from the app

- Slack: the Vespin bot can DM you each item to answer there. See [Slack](/slack).
- Mac and web: turn on **Play a sound** or **Show a notification** in **Settings › Preferences › Notifications**.
