---
title: "How the Inbox works"
description: "Answer your agents' decisions, to-dos, and questions in one place"
---

The Inbox is where your agents bring you what they can't do or decide alone. Each item is a **thread**: a title, the agent's messages, your replies, and usually one **ask**, the thing the agent needs from you. Open it from the sidebar or with <kbd>⇧⌘I</kbd>.

A thread belongs to a task. Every thread in your workspace's tasks lists in one Inbox, latest change first, so you never have to open each task to see what's waiting.

## The three asks

### Decide

The agent offers two to six options and may mark one **Recommended**. Click an option, or press its number (<kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd>), then **Send**. Some decisions let you pick several. When each option comes with pictures, there's also **None of these**.

A simple go-ahead shows two buttons, such as **Approve** and **Decline**.

### To-do

Something only you can do, such as changing a setting or adding a secret, as up to eight steps. Tick each step as you do it, then click **Mark done**, or **Couldn't do it** and say why in the note.

### Answer

A question the agent wants answered in your words, or a short form of fields: text, numbers, a choice of a few values, or a checkbox. Fields marked **Optional** can stay empty.

A thread with no ask is a notice, such as a pull request ready for review. Click **Got it** to settle it.

## Reply without answering

Write in the box under any thread and click **Send**. With nothing picked, your message goes to the agent and the ask stays open, so you can ask a question before deciding. A message also wakes an agent that has stopped. To quote part of a message, select it and press <kbd>R</kbd>.

**Add a note** under an ask sends your words with your answer.

## Who a thread waits on

A thread waits on the member the agent addressed it to, or else the task's assignee, or else whoever started the agent. Any member can still answer. A thread waiting on a teammate says **Waiting on** and their name.

## Choose what the Inbox lists

**View options** (the sliders above the list) chooses:

- **Questions**: open asks waiting on you.
- **Open**: everything you haven't finished with. The default.
- **All**: everything, settled threads included.
- **Reminders**: threads you set a reminder on.
- **Addressed to**: you, anyone, or one teammate.
- **Pinned tasks first**: lists your pinned tasks' threads above **Everything else**.

## Settle a thread

A thread is settled once you answer its ask, or once the agent withdraws the ask with a reason. To settle one without answering, click **Resolve** or press <kbd>E</kbd>. **Resolve** can be undone until the agent has been told.

If an agent posts to a settled thread again, it opens again.

To deal with a thread later, press <kbd>H</kbd> for **Remind me**. It leaves the list and comes back when the reminder is due.

## Keyboard

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Open the Inbox | <kbd>⇧⌘I</kbd> |
| Next or previous thread | <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd> |
| Choose an option | <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>9</kbd> |
| Reply to selection | <kbd>R</kbd> |
| Mark as read or unread | <kbd>U</kbd> |
| Remind me | <kbd>H</kbd> |
| Resolve | <kbd>E</kbd> |
| Open in task | <kbd>⌘O</kbd> |

## Notifications

- **Mac app and web app**: the sidebar's **Inbox** row counts threads waiting on you. To be told while Vespin is in the background, turn on **Show a notification** or **Play a sound** in **Settings › Preferences › Notifications**.
- **iPhone**: the thread waiting on you arrives as a notification you can answer from, with a button per option for a single-pick decision.
- **Slack**: Vespin's Slack bot sends threads to you as DMs. See [Slack](/slack).
