title: "Integrations"
description: "Connect your accounts at other services so your agents can use their tools"
---

An integration gives your agents the tools of an account you have at another service, such as Linear or Notion. Agents use those tools as you, so they can see and do what your account can.

## Connect an account

Integrations belong to you, not to a workspace. In **Settings › Integrations**, click **Connect** next to a service and sign in. If a sign-in expires later, click **Reconnect**.

The **GitHub** integration uses the GitHub account you sign in to Vespin with. It's separate from the GitHub connection in **Settings › Workspace**, which gives agents access to your repositories.

Connecting **Slack** also lets Vespin's Slack bot recognize you. See [Slack](/slack).

## More than one account

Some services let you connect more than one account, such as two Notion workspaces. Press **Add account** on the service's page, and use **Rename** in an account's **⋯** menu to tell them apart. When an agent isn't sure which account you mean, it asks before making changes.

## What agents get

By default, every agent you start can use every tool from your connected integrations, including tools that make changes. Automation runs get them too, since they run as you. To start new agents without an integration, switch it off under **MCP servers** in **Settings › Agents**.

To turn an integration off for one agent, open **MCP servers** from the **+** menu in its message box. You can also `@`-mention a server to give the agent access for its next turn only.

While the newest message to your agent is a teammate's, its calls to your integrations are refused. See [Handoff](/handoff).

Anything a tool returns, including a credential, ends up in the agent's transcript and is sent to its model.

## Disconnect

Press **Disconnect** at the foot of the service's page, or, with several accounts there, choose it from an account's **⋯** menu. Vespin revokes its access at the service, and agents lose the account on their next call. Other accounts at the same service stay connected.

From a terminal, use `vespin integrations`. See [Workspace](/cli/workspace).
