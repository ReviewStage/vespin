A task is one piece of work, from the sentence that starts it to its merged pull requests. It holds the work's agents and their pull requests, its sub-tasks, its pages, and its Inbox threads.

Press <kbd>⌘N</kbd> (<kbd>⌥⌘N</kbd> in a browser) to start one. Press <kbd>⌘⏎</kbd> on an empty box to make a task with no agent.

## The Overview

The task's name, at the top of the task, opens its **Overview**: its status, priority, and assignee, its sub-tasks, its agents, and its pages.

## Sub-tasks and relations

A sub-task is a task of its own that works in its parent's repository. A task can also be blocked by another. A block is advice: nothing stops a blocked task's agents from starting.

## Agents

Every agent on a task works in the task's repository. Press <kbd>⌘T</kbd> to start another.

## Pages

An agent can show you an interactive page, such as a chart, a table, or a mockup. A comment on a page goes to the agent that wrote it. **Start a review** to send several at once.

## The agent's browser {#the-agents-browser}

A cloud agent has its own browser, a Chromium in its sandbox, which it uses to check pages it built or to reach a site you sign in to. Open its tabs from **+** in the task's side panel. A tab the agent opens stays out of your panel until you do.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/tasks-browser.png" alt="An agent's Browser tab beside its conversation: Relay's accept page live, You're driving and Hand back over it, and the agent asking you to sign in for it" />
</Frame>

**Take over** to drive a tab yourself. The agent waits until you **Hand back**, or until 60 seconds after your last input.

To sign in for the agent, open a **New browser tab**, sign in, and hand it back. The cookies stay in that agent's sandbox for as long as it lasts.

Screenshots and recordings the agent takes appear in its conversation, which every member who can open the task can see.
