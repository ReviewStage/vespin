Member roles

Who may do what in a Relay workspace. Dana Moreno · v3 · November 2026

Roles

Owner. One per workspace. Holds billing and may transfer the workspace or delete it.

Admin. Manages members, invites, roles below their own, and webhooks.

Member. Works in the workspace; may invite teammates as members.

An invite link lasts 7 days and works once. A withdrawn or accepted link reads “This invite is no longer valid”.

Seats and billing

A pending invite holds no seat. A member counts toward the plan's seats from the moment they accept, and an admin sees the seat count change on the Members page.

Audit

Every invite sent, withdrawn, accepted, or expired is written to the workspace's audit log with who did it and when.
